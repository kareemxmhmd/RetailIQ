7. Methodological Notes & Limitations:
---------------------------------------
- Preprocessing: Monetary and Frequency are log-transformed (np.log1p) and scaled with StandardScaler,
  preventing wholesale outliers from collapsing the space into 2 clusters.
- Outlier Handling: Extreme values above the 99.9th percentile are clipped prior to transformation.
- Taxonomy Alignment: Segments are mapped to business personas via multi-dimensional RFM archetype matching.
- Model Limitation: KMeans assumes spherical clusters in Euclidean space. Highly correlated features or
  non-convex geometries should be monitored using the Population Stability Index (PSI) drift monitor.
